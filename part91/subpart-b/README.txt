AVIATION MICROLEARNING — PART 91 SUBPART B (DUAL-PATH PROTOTYPE)

Includes Part 1 (VFR) and Part 2 (IFR), each with ten illustrated, interactive screens; 13 short airport discoveries; and a full section-by-section index of the 52 substantive Subpart B regulations in the November 5, 2026 PDF.

TO OPEN ON A MAC: double-click index.html. If the browser blocks some local features, serve the folder locally by opening Terminal inside this directory and running:
python3 -m http.server 8000
Then open http://localhost:8000 in a browser.

FOR IPHONE/IPAD: deploy the ENTIRE folder (not just index.html) to a static HTTPS host. Example: https://app.netlify.com/drop, drag this unzipped folder. Open the resulting site URL in Safari, Share > Add to Home Screen.

This is a working prototype, not an FAA-approved course or substitute for current flight planning, an aircraft-approved manual, or flight instruction. All scenarios are simplified teaching examples. The VFR and IFR stories use fixed, invented flight facts for learning. The 13 side discoveries are short concept previews, not full proficiency lessons. Complete coverage of every section at teaching depth still requires additional lessons.

Offline service worker caches app shell and images; PDF may require an internet connection or direct file access depending on host/browser. PDF is included for source references.
