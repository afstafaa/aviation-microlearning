# Subpart A interaction design review — Version 2.1

The original ten-screen illustrated flight and all FAA reference panels are retained. The intent of this refinement is to reduce repetitive required clicks and make understanding come from visuals.

| Screen | Mode | Change | Must interact to continue? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Opening | Watch | Retain illustrated opening and staggered concepts | No |
| 2. Rules apply | Watch | Animated context-to-Part-91 diagram replaces tap-to-unlock text | No |
| 3. Who is PIC? | Explore | Tap either pilot to reveal the relationship between controls and authority | Yes, one meaningful role exploration |
| 4. Current annual | Watch | Animated logbook-vs-aircraft-condition comparison | No |
| 5. Inspect the defect | Explore | Locate structural defect and uncover airworthiness issue | Yes |
| 6. Operating limits | Explore | Weight gauge visualizes an example approved limit; adjust load | Yes |
| 7. Rule connections | Watch | Auto-unfolding flow links 91.7 → 91.9 → 91.3 | No |
| 8. In-flight emergency | Decide | One applied choice with feedback | Yes, correct choice |
| 9. After landing | Explore | Compare report mechanisms, optional detail reveals | No |
| 10. Transfer challenge | Decide | Apply the airworthiness idea to a different aircraft | Yes, correct choice |

**Source scope:** Based on the Nov 5, 2026 Part 91 PDF. Scenario conditions and weight figures are illustrative; this does not replace current regulatory or aircraft guidance. The seven airport discoveries are retained unchanged for a later review.

**Artwork:** Scene illustrations are unchanged and still require a dedicated C172 accuracy pass (including the single, two-blade nose-mounted propeller).
